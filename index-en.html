A simple media player for your own sources

Melyro is a media player for iPhone and iPad. It plays the source you add yourself — and nothing else.

Melyro includes no content. There are no channels, films, series, playlists or provider directory in the app. Melyro is not affiliated with any content provider.

Only use sources you have the right to watch.

What can it play?

M3U playlist

A file from the Files app, or a web address (URL).

Xtream-compatible login

The server address, username and password your provider gave you.

Jellyfin / Emby

Your own media server.

Privacy at a glance

  • The developer runs no servers and has no accounts, ads or analytics of its own.
  • Your source addresses, watch history and settings stay on your device; passwords are kept in the iOS Keychain.
  • Chromecast support uses the Google Cast SDK; see the privacy policy for details.
