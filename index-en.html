Bring the source.Melyro does the rest.

Your M3U playlist, Xtream login or Jellyfin and Emby server. Watch on iPhone and iPad: dark, fast and ad-free.

Download on the App Store How it works

Three sources, one player.

  1. M3U playlist

    A file from Files or an address. The TV guide comes from the address your playlist names.

  2. Xtream login

    The server, username and password your provider gave you. The password stays in the iOS Keychain.

  3. Jellyfin and Emby

    The films and series on your own server, from where you left off.

Melyro includes no content. There are no channels, films, series or provider directory in the app, and Melyro is not affiliated with any provider. Only add sources you have the right to watch.

Live TV, films and series.In one place.

What's on, at a glance.

The programme on air, when it ends and how far along, on every channel.

Change channel without leaving.

The next channels sit right under the picture.

Right where you left off.

As an episode ends, the next one is offered. It can play on its own.

Wide on iPad. Split on iPhone Duo.

Send it to the big screen.
